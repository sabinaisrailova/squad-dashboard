Ladder in this simulation: Product Owner (grade 10) → Senior Product Owner (11) → Expert Product Owner (12) — three IC levels, each in a Business PO or Technical PO track. Delivery Lead is a parallel role at the tribe level with its own KPI set, not a fourth rung on the IC ladder: moving into it is not a promotion and does not require passing the Expert gate. Promotion gates: G0 eligibility (tenure and results), G1 assessment centre, G2 360 and individual KPI, G3 panel interview with a Bar Raiser. PO composite = 40% product outcome (percentile within archetype × contribution quality filter) + 30% AMI + 30% competency/AC. Delivery Lead composite = 40% delivery health + 30% team & talent + 30% portfolio outcome. Delivery and flow metrics never feed into Product Owner KPI — they feed into Delivery Lead KPI, where they are a direct responsibility.
